Help

Plain answers for the main jobs. Still stuck? Email us — a person reads it.

How do I capture extra work in the field?

Open Capture T&M Ticket, pick the job, name who must approve, describe the change, and add labor/materials lines. Your markup is applied automatically. Tap the photo boxes to attach condition photos, then Price this change order — it lands in the CO Log as “Priced.”

How does the customer sign without an account?

In the CO Log, tap Send for signature on a priced change order. Your customer gets an email link (a “magic link”) that opens the priced change order on their phone — no app, no account, no password. They type their name, check the agreement box, and sign. The log flips to “Approved” with their name and the time.

What do the five states mean?

Priced — captured and priced, not yet sent. Submitted — sent to the customer/GC, awaiting approval. Approved — signed; safe to do the work, must still be billed. Billed — on an invoice/pay application. Paid — money in. PaidChange exists so nothing dies quietly between those states.

How does chasing work?

The Chase tab lists anything stuck — submitted 7+ days, approved-but-unbilled 5+ days, billed-unpaid 30+ days — each with a ready-to-send email you can edit. Mark it chased and the clock resets.

How do I get this into QuickBooks?

Use Export CSV in the CO Log: one row per change order with job, customer, amount, and state — your bookkeeper imports or keys it into the next QuickBooks invoice, and Print / PDF log gives you the paper copy. Direct QuickBooks Online sync is a later phase; the export is the bridge today.

Where does my data live?

In this demo, everything lives in your own browser on this device (local storage) — sample data is fictional. Use Reset demo data in the CO Log to restore the samples, or Clear demo data to empty the ledger. In the live product your company ledger is backed up and exportable at any time.
